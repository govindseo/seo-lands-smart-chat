An AI chat assistant for WordPress. It reads your site's pages, posts, products, courses and custom post types, then answers visitor questions in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Works on online stores, service or course sites, or both. It can detect the site type on its own.

**Download:** [wordpress.org/plugins/seo-lands-smart-chat](https://wordpress.org/plugins/seo-lands-smart-chat/)

## Features

- **Store and service modes** – product cards with price, rating and add-to-cart on stores; advisor-style replies on service sites
- **AI engines** – Google Gemini or Grok (xAI), with Groq keys also supported, automatic backup and model selection. A built-in search works without any API key
- **Answer rules** – custom AI instructions, blocked topics, and a price policy that either shows prices from your site or shares contact details instead
- **Live chat inbox** – take over a chat, reply as a team, show online status
- **Email alerts** – optional alerts to admins for offline messages and to visitors on replies
- **Lead capture** – into your own form (Elementor Pro, Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms or any form shortcode), plus an optional CRM webhook
- **SEO-aware** – skips noindex / nofollow, landing and thank-you pages
- **Contact detection** – finds your support phone, WhatsApp and email, and answers contact questions instantly
- **Lightweight** – cache-friendly, mobile-friendly and cookie-free

## Requirements

- WordPress 5.8 or higher
- PHP 7.4 or higher

## Installation

1. In WordPress, go to **Plugins → Add New** and search for **SEO Lands Smart Chat**.
2. Install and activate it.
3. Open the plugin settings, add a Gemini or Grok API key (optional), and choose your site type.

## FAQ

**Do I need an API key?**
No. The built-in search works without one. Add a Gemini or Grok key for full AI answers.

**Does it work without WooCommerce or courses?**
Yes. It works on any WordPress site, including service and content sites.

**The assistant gave a wrong price. How do I stop that?**
Use the price policy setting to share contact details instead of prices.
